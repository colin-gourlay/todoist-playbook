# Willo Video Review and Candidate Screening

A structured workflow for reviewing asynchronous video submissions from Willo as part of an early-stage recruitment process. Covers preparation, consistent video review, candidate evaluation, shortlisting, and follow-up actions.

---

## Objective

- Apply consistent evaluation criteria across all candidates
- Capture structured notes per candidate during and after review
- Assess communication clarity, confidence, and relevance of responses
- Categorise candidates into shortlist, maybe, and no progression
- Produce a final shortlist ready for next-stage interviews

Estimated duration: 25 minutes (excluding video watch time).

---

## When to Use

- When reviewing a batch of Willo video responses for an open role
- As part of any asynchronous video screening process
- When you need a repeatable, bias-reducing review workflow for early-stage candidate screening

---

## Structure Overview

1. Preparation - review criteria and confirm submissions are in
2. Video Review - watch each video and capture structured notes
3. Candidate Evaluation - score and rank candidates against criteria
4. Shortlisting - categorise candidates and confirm the final shortlist
5. Follow-up Actions - communicate outcomes and schedule next steps

---

## Import Instructions

1. Download `template.csv`
2. Create a new project in Todoist
3. Import from CSV
4. Rename project to: `Willo Review - [Role Name]`
5. Work through each section in order for every candidate batch
