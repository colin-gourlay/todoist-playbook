# Album of the Week (Weekly Review)

Weekly workflow for selecting, listening to, and reviewing an album - from discovery through to broadcast and promotion.

This template is designed to ensure every album review follows a consistent process, reducing the chance of missed steps and improving the quality and repeatability of each review.

---

## Objective

- Select and research the Album of the Week
- Listen critically and capture detailed notes
- Prepare review commentary for broadcast
- Deliver the review on air and publish supporting content
- Promote the album across social channels

---

## When to Use

- At the start of each week when an Album of the Week segment is planned
- As a standalone review checklist for any album deep-dive
- When onboarding a new presenter or producer to the review process

---

## Structure Overview

1. Preparation
2. Listening & Notes
3. Review Preparation
4. Broadcast & Content
5. Promotion

---

## Import Instructions

1. Download `template.csv`
2. Create a new project in Todoist
3. Import from CSV
4. Rename the project to: `Album of the Week – [Artist – Album Title]`
5. Work through each section in order throughout the week leading up to broadcast
